Study Profile

The CHICO study manuscript describes a longitudinal multi-omics cohort built to resolve how cervical microbiome dynamics relate to HPV infection, viral persistence, and cervical cancer progression. This app presents the cohort in an editorial-style interface for rapid reading, comparison, and hypothesis generation.

In the manuscript draft, the CHICO project integrates retrospective epidemiology from 226,240 cervical screening records (2014-2023, Chengdu) with a prospective cohort of 2,447 women, including 2,218 HPV-positive participants and 229 HPV-negative controls. This design connects population-scale prevalence patterns with longitudinal microbiome and host molecular readouts.

The draft highlights that baseline cervical microbiome composition differs by HPV status and risk category, with enrichment of taxa such as Brucella, Gardnerella, and Sphingomonas in HPV-positive groups, and depletion of protective Lactobacillus in dysbiotic states. Follow-up analyses further distinguish persistent from non-persistent infection trajectories.

Beyond 16S profiling, the manuscript frames CHICO as a multi-omics resource: metagenomics and metabolomics are used to explore microbiome-mediated metabolic shifts, and single-nucleus RNA-seq is used to examine epithelial and immune cellular states linked to HPV. Together, these layers support a mechanistic view of persistence and carcinogenesis rather than correlation alone.

In this app, the visualization desk exposes the most directly explorable microbiome comparisons from the manuscript draft, including single-taxon distribution comparisons and Top-N compositional summaries across user-selected HPV grouping schemes (status, risk, persistence). The surrounding pages summarize study rationale, cohort structure, and the contributing team.

"CHICO links epidemiology, cervical microbiome dynamics, and host multi-omics profiles to study why only a subset of HPV infections persist and progress."

How To Read The App

Editorial Structure

The About page summarizes the manuscript narrative (rationale, cohort, and major findings), the Data Visualization page provides an interactive subset of microbiome comparisons, and the Authors page maps the manuscript roster to institutions and correspondence notes.

Analysis Workflow

  • Select a display method (single-taxon distribution or Top-N stacked composition).
  • Choose taxonomic level, HPV grouping type, and one or more groups to compare.
  • Generate the figure and inspect either a non-parametric test summary (single taxon) or a composition table (Top-N mode).
